No accountWe never ask for your name, email or phone number.
Camera stays on deviceNever recorded, saved, uploaded or shared.

02Camera and face data

Two features ask for permission to use your front camera:

  • Prank Face: camera frames are analysed in real time, on your device, with Google ML Kit on-device face detection. It finds where your eyes, nose and mouth are so the filter lines up with your face.
  • Prank Call: during a simulated video call, your own camera picture is shown in the corner of the screen, like in a real video call.

Camera images and face positions exist only while the screen is open. They are not recorded, not saved, not sent to us or anyone else, and never used to identify you. The app does not perform facial recognition.

ML Kit may send Google basic technical metrics about how the component performs; these never include camera images. You can refuse or withdraw camera permission at any time in your device settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

04What the app never accesses

The app does not access your contacts, call log, messages, photos, files, microphone or precise location. Calls are simulated inside the app: no real call is placed, the phone's calling features are not used, and the microphone button in a call is only part of the pretend screen.

05Information stored on your device

To load faster and use less data, videos you watch are kept for a while in the app's cache, up to about 400 MB. Older videos are removed automatically. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes this cache.

06Sharing

Information is shared only with the providers listed above so that they can provide their services. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it. These providers process data under their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps
  • Firebase privacy and security
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • Mintegral Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Liftoff Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

These providers may process information in countries other than your own.

07Your choices

  • Ad consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other places where it is required, the app asks for your choice with Google's consent form before loading ads. You can change it at any time with the privacy options button at the top of the home screen.
  • Personalized ads: on Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google or Privacy settings. On iOS, use Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Camera: turn the permission off in your device settings.
  • Stop all collection: uninstall the app.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because the app has no accounts, we usually cannot link data to you by name, but contact us and we will help as far as we can.

08Data retention

Camera images are not kept at all. The video cache stays on your device as described above. Data collected by Google, its ad partners and Cloudflare is kept according to their own retention policies.

09Security

Videos are delivered over encrypted HTTPS connections with short-lived access tokens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the information the app handles.

10Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will take appropriate action.
