Privacy Policy

Prank Verse · Effective November 10, 2026

Prank Verse (“the app”, “we”) is a mobile entertainment app with funny videos, simulated phone calls, face filters and screen pranks. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • There is no account or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your camera image stays on your device. It is never saved, uploaded or shared.
  • The app shows ads and uses analytics and crash reporting from Google, which collect device-level data described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Camera and face data

The Prank Face feature asks for permission to use your camera. Camera frames are analysed in real time, on your device, using Google ML Kit on-device face detection. This finds the position of facial features, such as eyes and mouth, so that filters line up with your face.

  • Camera images and face positions are used only while the filter is on screen.
  • They are not saved to your device, not sent to us or to anyone else, and not used to identify you.
  • The app does not perform facial recognition.

ML Kit may send Google basic technical metrics about how the component performs. These metrics do not include camera images. You can refuse or withdraw camera permission at any time in your device settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

3. What the app does not access

The app does not access your contacts, call log, messages, photos, files, microphone or precise location. Fake calls are simulated inside the app: no real call is placed, and the phone's calling features are not used.

4. Information stored on your device

To reduce loading and data use, videos you watch are temporarily kept in the app's cache, up to about 400 MB. Older videos are removed automatically. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes this cache.

5. Sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers listed above so that they can provide their services. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law. These providers process data under their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Firebase privacy and security
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

These providers may process information in countries other than your own.

6. Your choices

  • Ad consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your choice before loading ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time with the privacy options button at the top of the home screen.
  • Personalized ads: on Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google or Privacy settings. On iOS, you can control tracking under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Camera: turn the permission off in your device settings.
  • Stop all collection: uninstall the app.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because the app has no accounts, we generally cannot link data to you by name. Contact us and we will help as far as we can. You may also use the tools Google provides for your Google account and advertising settings.

7. Data retention

Camera images are not retained. The video cache stays on your device as described above. Data collected by Google and Cloudflare is kept according to their own retention policies and settings.

8. Security

Video requests use encrypted HTTPS connections with short-lived access tokens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the information the app handles.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so that we can take appropriate action.
